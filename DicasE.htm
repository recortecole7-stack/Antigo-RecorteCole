Some tips that help you assembling the models.

Tools

  • Scissor.
  • Glue (white).
  • Toothpick.

    Assembling

  • Read previously the instructions (if available) and see carefully the pictures, before assembling.
  • After cutting the piece, fold before pasting it.
  • You will probably spend some hours assembling the models. Be patient.

    Printing

  • I suggest using 90 to 120 g/m2 papers. The drawings were originally prepared to A4 paper size.
  • You should configure your printer setting to increase the printing quality.
  • The old files are BMP format, compacted by WinZip. You can use PAINT to open and print it.
  • The new files are PDF (Adobe Acrobat). Click the button below to free download if you don't have Acrobat Reader.
